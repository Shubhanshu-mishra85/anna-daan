## Ek scene socho

Raat ke 11 baje. Shaadi khatam. 80 logon ka dal-chawal, sabzi aur mithai bach gayi hai.
Halwai bolta hai, *"Bhaiya, ab ye fenkna padega."*

Wahi se 2 km door kuch log aaj raat bhookhe soye hain.

**Khana waha hai. Zarooratmand yaha hai. Beech me koi nahi hai.**
Anna Daan wahi "beech wala" hai.

## Ye kaam kaise karta hai

| Kaun | Kya karta hai | Time |
|---|---|---|
| 🧑‍🍳 **Host** | Jagah, khana (veg/non-veg), kitne log ka, kab bana, phone number daalta hai | 30 second |
| 🙋 **Volunteer / NGO** | Live list dekhta hai, **"Main utha lunga"** dabata hai ya seedha call karta hai | 1 tap |
| 🍽️ **Zarooratmand** | Garam, safe khana milta hai | Timer ke andar |

---

## Khaas baat: khana ek ghadi ke saath aata hai

Har post ke saath ek **ghoomta hua timer ring** hota hai. Jaise time bachta kam hota hai, ring chhota hota jaata hai, aur **1 ghante se kam bachne pe laal** ho jaata hai.

| Khana | Safe time |
|---|---|
| 🥗 Veg (dal, chawal, sabzi, roti) | **6 ghante** |
| 🍗 Non-veg | **4 ghante** |

- Isse zyada purana khana **post hi nahi hota**.
- Time khatam hote hi post **apne aap hat jaata hai**.
- Matlab kisi ko baasi khana jaane ka khatra nahi.

## Aur kya-kya hai

- 🍛 **Interactive thaali:** home page ki katori dabao, us khane ka safe time dikhega
- 🔎 **Filter:** Sab / Veg / Non-veg

- 🌗 **Dark mode** aur phone pe bhi poora sahi dikhta hai
- 🗣️ **Hinglish me likha hai**, jo har kisi ko samajh aaye

## Chalao apne computer pe

```bash

cd anna-daan
```

## GitHub Pages pe live karo

1. Repo me `index.html` aur `README.md` daalo

3. 1-2 minute me link ready
